Synthetic STL/WAV fixtures under `tests/fixtures/` are generated by this project;
they contain no study material or third-party assets. Their original SHA-256
values are recorded in `tests/fixtures/sha256.json`. GitHub currently rejects LFS
uploads for this repository, so the fixtures are generated locally and in CI;
the push/fresh-clone comparison remains blocked in #43.

Use `python tools/fetch-asset.py --url <immutable-https-url> --sha256 <reviewed-hash>
--output external-assets/<file>` only after reviewing the source/license. It
refuses missing hashes, mutable revisions, overwrites and outputs outside the
ignored asset directory. This is intentionally not a bulk download of USDs.
